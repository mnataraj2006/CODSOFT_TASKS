# Data Visualization Report

## Dataset Overview
This report details the data visualization analysis performed on the cleaned retail sales dataset (`cleaned_data.csv`) consisting of **5,000 observations** and **12 columns**.
* **Domain:** Transactional retail sales records spanning from Jan 1, 2022 to Sep 9, 2035 (exactly one sale per day).
* **Objective:** Present descriptive, temporal, and relational analysis through highly polished visualizations (comparisons, trends, shares, distributions, and scatter metrics) to extract business insights and establish the dashboard layout.

---

## 1. Bar Chart: Category Sales Performance
* **Visualized Metrics:** Total revenue generated by `product_category` (with text labels).
* **Justification for Chart Selection:** A vertical bar chart is the most effective way to rank and compare discrete categorical segments by an aggregate metric.
* **Key Finding:** **Electronics** is the leading sales revenue generator, contributing **$1,829,899.22** (35.8% of total sales). Clothing is second with **$1,531,931.72** (30.0%), followed by Home ($982,083.92) and Beauty ($765,860.88). Electronics and Clothing drive 65.8% of total revenue.
* **File Location:** [bar_chart.png](file:///c:/Users/DELL/Desktop/CodSoft/CODSOFT_TASK3/charts/bar_chart.png)

## 2. Line Chart: Monthly Chronological Sales Trend
* **Visualized Metrics:** Total transaction revenue grouped chronologically by month (`year_month`).
* **Justification for Chart Selection:** A line chart is standard and required to track a continuous metric over an ordered time dimension, showing fluctuations, trends, and seasonal cycles.
* **Key Finding:** Sales revenue exhibits a highly stable **stationary demand cycle**, fluctuating consistently between **$20,000 and $45,000** monthly. There are no major long-term growth or decline trends, showing stable business demand.
* **File Location:** [line_chart.png](file:///c:/Users/DELL/Desktop/CodSoft/CODSOFT_TASK3/charts/line_chart.png)

---

## 3. Pie Chart: Proportions of Transactions by Payment Method
* **Visualized Metrics:** Transaction count shares across `payment_method` categories.
* **Justification for Chart Selection:** A pie/donut chart is appropriate when showing parts-of-a-whole shares for a manageable number of categories (3 categories: Card, COD, Wallet).
* **Key Finding:** **Card** is the preferred payment option representing **45.4%** of all transactions (2,270 transactions), followed by Cash on Delivery (COD) at **35.5%** (1,774 transactions), and Wallet at **19.1%** (956 transactions).
* **File Location:** [pie_chart.png](file:///c:/Users/DELL/Desktop/CodSoft/CODSOFT_TASK3/charts/pie_chart.png)

---

## 4. Histogram: Distribution of Transaction Revenue
* **Visualized Metrics:** Sales revenue distribution grouped into 40 bins with overlaid mean and median markers.
* **Justification for Chart Selection:** Histograms are the standard visualization to represent the frequency distribution of a continuous variable.
* **Key Finding:** Revenue has a **moderately right-skewed distribution** (skewness = 1.00), where the median transaction value is **$796.65** and the mean value is **$1,021.96**. This represents a concentration of lower-to-mid value orders with a long tail of high-value purchases.
* **File Location:** [histogram.png](file:///c:/Users/DELL/Desktop/CodSoft/CODSOFT_TASK3/charts/histogram.png)

## 5. Scatter Plot: Price vs. Revenue by Category
* **Visualized Metrics:** Unit Price on the X-axis vs. Revenue on the Y-axis, color-coded by `product_category`.
* **Justification for Chart Selection:** A scatter plot is the primary visualization to assess individual relationship patterns between two continuous variables.
* **Key Finding:** There is a strong positive correlation of **0.678** between Unit Price and Revenue, indicating that item price points directly scale transaction values. Points for all categories are mixed evenly, confirming that item pricing is uniform across all categories.
* **File Location:** [scatter_plot.png](file:///c:/Users/DELL/Desktop/CodSoft/CODSOFT_TASK3/charts/scatter_plot.png)

## 7. Key Visual Insights
1. **Electronics is the lead product category:** Yielded the highest sales revenue ($1.83M, or 35.8% share).
2. **Card is the preferred checkout method:** Accounts for 45.4% of transaction checkouts.
3. **No Promo Leverage:** The correlation between discount rate and quantity ordered is near zero (-0.004), showing that price discounts do not drive larger cart sizes.
4. **Stable Demand Cycle:** Monthly sales show a stable stationary fluctuation pattern between $20k and $45k.
5. **Uniform Operations:** Average delivery days (~6.12) and customer ratings (~2.97) are consistent across all categories and regions.

## 8. Dashboard Plan (Power BI Design)
The dashboard is designed for high impact and interactivity:
* **KPI Summary Cards:** Total Sales ($5.11M), Transactions (5,000), Avg Customer Rating (2.97), and Avg Delivery Days (6.12).
* **Main Visuals:** Revenue by Product Category (Clustered Column Chart), Monthly Sales Trend (Line Chart), and Payment Method breakdown (Donut Chart).
* **Secondary Visual:** Transaction-level Scatter Plot (Unit Price vs. Revenue).
* **Interactive Slicers:** Region, Product Category, and Transaction Date range slider.
* **Calculations:** Implemented using exact DAX measures as described in the Power BI Instructions.

---

## 9. Conclusion
The visual analysis confirms a stable, uniform, and healthy retail operation. Revenue is driven directly by item pricing and cart size (quantity), with steady demand over the years. Promotional discounts do not stimulate larger quantity orders, offering a key margin optimization opportunity. The planned Power BI dashboard layout will provide stakeholders with the interactive tools to filter and monitor these core metrics in real-time.
